This is to inform all students that the Pre-Registration and Registration Confirmation process for Spring 2026. All students must carefully follow the instructions below:

  1. Students must complete their Pre-Registration within the scheduled Pre-Registration Dates.
  2. Once you submit Pre-Registration You may edit and resubmit within the deadline. After the deadline, no changes can be made.
  3. If you do not complete Pre-Registration → You cannot submit to Advisor.
  4. If you pre-registered but did not submit to Advisor → You will be treated as Not Registered.
  5. If you completed both Pre-Registration and Advisor Submission but did not pay within the payment deadline → You will be Not Registered.
  6. During Pre-Registration, Maximum credit is based on the most recent semester with GPA > 0. After Result Publication, Maximum credit is based on the newly earned GPA.
  7. After Semester Results, prerequisite rules will apply. And if GPA increases → You may add/drop courses. If GPA decreases → You must reduce credit to match allowed limit. Advisor Submission will remain blocked until credit matches your GPA limit.
  8. After submitting it to Advisor, Students cannot make changes. Advisors may adjust before the advisor's deadline. Missing Advisor submission → counted as Not Registered.
  9. After advising is over, ACFD will publish the payment notice and payment dates. Student portals will display: "Registration Confirmation Payment Required" with total payable amount. Students must pay within the given deadline.
  10. If Payment Is Not Made: Pre-Registration will be CANCELLED, Advisor Confirmation will be CANCELLED and The semester will be marked as DROP.
  11. Failure at any stage → Not Registered.

General Examination Conduct and Eligibility Criteria:

  1. Admit Cards must be downloaded and printed prior to exam entry.
  2. Minimum 75% class attendance required to qualify for Midterm and Final Exams.
  3. Cell phones, smartwatches, and programmable devices are strictly prohibited in exam halls.
  4. Students acquiring GPA below 2.00 in 3 consecutive semesters will face academic probation/readmission.
