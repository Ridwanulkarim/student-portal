This is to inform all students that the Pre-Registration and Registration Confirmation process for Spring 2026: All students must carefully follow the instructions below:

  1. Students must complete their Pre-Registration within the scheduled Pre-Registration Dates.
  2. Once submitted, you can edit and resubmit within the deadline. After deadline, no changes can be made.
  3. If you do not complete Pre-Registration → You cannot submit to Advisor.
  4. If you pre-registered but did not submit to Advisor → You will be treated as Not Registered.
  5. If you completed both Pre-Registration and Advisor Submission but did not pay within the deadline → You will Not be Registered.
  6. During Pre-Registration, Maximum credit is based on the most recent semester GPA. If GPA > 0, After Result Publication, Maximum credit is based on newly earned GPA.
  7. After Semester Results, prerequisite rules will apply. And if GPA increases → You may add/drop courses. If GPA decreases → You must reduce credit to match allowed limit. Advisor Submission will remain Blocked until credit matches your GPA limit.
  8. After submitting to Advisor, Students cannot make changes. Advisors may adjust before deadline. Missing Advisor submission → counted as Not Registered.
  9. If Payment Is Not Made: Pre-Registration will be CANCELLED and the semester will be marked as DROP.
  10. Failure at any stage → Not Registered
